# Quality Upcycler / 品质转化机

使用品质插件提升物品品质，也可能损毁物品。分拣并回流低品质产物，继续加工。

研究品质插件1后解锁。接电、插入品质插件，再用机械臂进出料；无需手选配方。支持材料、建筑、弹药、武器、护甲、装备、修理包、科研瓶和载具。蓝图与规划工具不参与加工。

机器占地3×3，有4个品质插件槽和12格输出空间。速度与回收机相同，工作功率360 kW，待机12 kW，运行合计372 kW。每分钟污染4。

产物可能保持原品质、提升一级或多级，也可能损毁；品质插件越好，升级机会越高。已达已解锁最高品质的物品会原样输出。没有品质插件时不会升品，仍会损毁物品。

幸存物保留剩余弹药、耐久比例、损伤、标签、内部物品和已安装装备；内部装备不会随外层护甲或载具一起升品。物品损毁时，其内容物也随之损毁。易腐物不会被刷新新鲜度。

---

Upgrade items using quality modules, with a risk of destruction. Sort and return lower-quality outputs for another pass.

Unlocked by Quality module 1. Connect power, insert quality modules, then feed and extract items with inserters. Recipes are selected automatically. Supports materials, buildings, ammunition, weapons, armor, equipment, repair packs, science packs and vehicles. Blueprints and planning tools are excluded.

3×3 footprint, four quality-module slots and twelve output slots. Recycler crafting speed. Active power: 360 kW, idle drain: 12 kW, total while working: 372 kW. Pollution: 4/min.

An item can survive unchanged, gain one or more quality tiers, or be destroyed. Better quality modules improve the odds. Items at the highest unlocked quality pass through safely. Without quality modules, items cannot upgrade and can still be destroyed.

Survivors retain ammunition, durability percentage, damage, tags, stored items and installed equipment. Contents keep their original qualities and are lost if the containing item is destroyed. Spoilable items do not become fresh again.
